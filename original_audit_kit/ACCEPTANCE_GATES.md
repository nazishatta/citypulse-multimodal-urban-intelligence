# Audit decision gates

These are **recommended engineering targets**, NOT professor-authored grading thresholds.

**Blockers (NO-GO or conditional scope change):** No lawful/useful access to actual photos if image analysis is required; no viable shared-reviewer network; uninterpretable key joins, dates or locations; unacceptable public-use terms; public-deployment cannot load sample safely.

**Provisional working targets to test rather than assume:** >=200 mapped in-scope businesses, >=3,000 dated nonempty in-scope reviews, >=100 repeat reviewers across distinct in-scope businesses, and >=100 viewable authorized images linked to >=20 in-scope businesses. At least 24 observed months for meaningful time-series comparison. Relax or increase scientifically with evidence, not just for convenience.

**Midterm GO:** actual verified multimodal data; at least 3 meaningful interactive modalities and best-effort coverage for 1–10; scientific captions, accessible equivalents, deterministic generation, reproducible deployed app. Aim for all 6 key modalities to prepare for final.

**Final GO:** Week 11 API, Week 12 serving backend, Week 13 model analysis, Week 14 accessible deployment/ethics, evaluated end-to-end.

**Unknown ≠ PASS.** Missing photo assets remain NOT VERIFIED, never passed due to photo metadata alone.
